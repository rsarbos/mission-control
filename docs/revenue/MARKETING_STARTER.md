# RSARBOS Marketing Starter

## Positioning

From property link to investor-ready decision.

RSARBOS turns a listing link into a structured underwriting dossier with comps, rental logic, ARV thesis, risk notes, pricing context, and a decision summary.

## Short Outreach Message

Hey, I’m launching RSARBOS manual underwriting reports for investors, flippers, and acquisition teams.

Send a property link and RSARBOS turns it into an investor-ready dossier: property overview, comps, ARV thesis, rental logic, risk notes, pricing context, and decision summary.

Launch pricing is $100/report while we validate the manual workflow before the automated underwriting core goes live.

## Audience Angles

First-time investors:
Know what you are looking at before writing an offer.

Flippers and operators:
Pressure-test ARV, rehab assumptions, comps, and deal-killing risks.

Acquisition teams:
Standardize how opportunities are reviewed before they reach decision-makers.

Capital partners:
Turn property logic into a clean document that lenders, partners, and stakeholders can review.
