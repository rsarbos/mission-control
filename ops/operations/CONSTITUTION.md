# CONSTITUTION — AXIOM-OPS (Operations)

Inherits from: /axiom/AXIOM_CONSTITUTION.md

## Purpose
Operational practices that keep the organization running and the founder focused.

## Operations Addendum
- Runbooks for critical paths and incidents.
- Playbooks for onboarding, offboarding, and routine ops.
- Clear SLAs for monitoring and alerting.
- Prioritize automation that reduces founder cognitive load.
