## Repository Boundary

RSARBOS is an evidence-first real estate underwriting operating system. The current product is a manual underwriting dossier delivered by humans. Mission Control is the founder/operator surface. RSARBOS Core is the future deterministic, versioned truth engine for calculations, replay, evidence normalization, and governed automation.

Manual Underwriting and Core are two execution modes of the same eventual system:

- Manual mode captures customer demand, source custody, fulfillment workflow, assumptions, approvals, costs, and feedback.
- Core mode eventually turns stable, validated patterns into deterministic functions and governed evidence workflows.

Mission Control must not calculate financial truth, invent evidence, autonomously approve dossiers, or become Core. It may record which inputs, assumptions, evidence, and build artifacts were used so that Core can later receive cleaner, validated data.

## Revenue-First Guardrails

A feature is in scope only when it directly supports at least one of:

- finding prospects;
- contacting prospects;
- following up;
- converting prospects;
- collecting payment;
- fulfilling a paid dossier;
- delivering a dossier;
- measuring customer behavior;
- reducing fulfillment time or cost;
- creating reusable evidence for future Core automation.

Deferred unless explicitly reactivated by constitutional documents:

- nationwide zoning automation;
- universal document parsing;
- autonomous investment approval;
- advanced agent orchestration;
- automated photo-to-invoice repair estimation;
- full commercial underwriting;
- portfolio intelligence;
- broad marketplace functionality;
- academy functionality;
- lead-selling infrastructure;
- sophisticated investor portals;
- speculative enterprise controls.

## Current Commercial Truth

The current product has not yet recorded a paid sale in Mission Control. Empty states must say this plainly and guide the founder toward the next action. Seed or sample data must be labeled sample/demo and must not create fake revenue, fake customers, fake conversions, fake testimonials, or fake performance claims.

Commercial learning should be captured without distracting from direct selling. Mission Control should explicitly track buyer segment, channel, message, package, price, sales cycle, turnaround, dossier sections that influenced purchase, dossier sections that influenced property acquisition, fulfillment economics, and repeat purchase behavior.

## AXIOM Role

AXIOM is the repository and execution steward. In this phase AXIOM may enforce document awareness, repository health, required reading order, implementation scope, architecture boundaries, unresolved placeholders, broken routes, migration safety, build/test status, and deferred-feature discipline.

AXIOM must not claim to perform capabilities that are not implemented. AXIOM does not calculate financial truth, invent evidence, or autonomously approve dossiers.
